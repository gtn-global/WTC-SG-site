From Local Manufacturing to a Building-Materials Network across Africa, Central Asia and Southeast Asia

This is a listed home & building materials company that grew from local manufacturing into global markets, with products sold across Europe, the Americas, Africa, Oceania, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and beyond. As the consulting team on this project, we provide the market-entry framework and local resource connections for its simultaneous expansion across Africa, Central Asia and Southeast Asia.

Client Listed home & building materials company · Key regions Africa · Central Asia · Southeast Asia · Products in 6 continents

Client Background

This is a home & building materials company founded in the 1990s and headquartered in one of China's well-known building-materials clusters. It was the first listed company in its domestic segment. The company focuses on R&D and manufacturing of smart locks, security doors and smart-home products, and is among the drafting units of national and industry standards for doors and locks, with hundreds of authorized national patents.

On the manufacturing side, the company operates a national-level 5G "future factory" and multiple smart-manufacturing bases, with strong digital, scaled delivery capability. Internationally, its products are sold across Europe, the Americas, Africa, Oceania, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and beyond, and the company has taken part in overseas construction projects such as housing programmes in the Middle East, while also exhibiting at regional infrastructure shows to deepen its international footprint.

"From local manufacturing to the world stage, this company is winning the trust of global customers with the strength of Chinese smart manufacturing."

— Dong Lixin (Bruce Dong)

The Challenge and Our Role

When a mature manufacturer moves from "exporting products" to "deepening regional markets," the real difficulty is rarely capacity — it is understanding local channels, certification standards, engineering procurement chains and partner networks. Africa, Central Asia and Southeast Asia differ sharply in market conditions, infrastructure tempo and procurement habits, so simply replicating the domestic playbook does not work.

As the consulting team on this internationalization project, we help map market-entry paths, connect local partnership resources, and build channel and service frameworks suited to each regional market.

"Going global is not just about selling products abroad — it is about building a system that can take root locally."

— Dong Lixin (Bruce Dong)
