From China to Global Markets — Going Global with Industrial Autonomous Forklifts

An industrial autonomous forklift and smart logistics robotics company wanted to use Singapore as a regional operations hub to complete overseas market entry and global expansion. Here is the full story of that journey.

Smart Logistics · Robotics China → Global Markets GlobalX Accelerator Regional Operations Hub

Operations hub launched in 8 months · 3 overseas markets entered · 1 round of industry capital connected

Background and Challenge

The company had built mature navigation, scheduling and safety technology for industrial autonomous forklifts, with proven product and delivery capability at home. But overseas target markets' warehousing standards, floor conditions and labor-compliance requirements differ sharply, so the product could not be copied directly. Locally, system integrators who understood autonomous-forklift scenarios were scarce, making the sales-to-delivery chain long and the payment cycle unpredictable.

Leadership determined that to genuinely capture the smart-logistics demand from overseas manufacturing upgrades, the company needed a regional node with demo, tuning and spare-parts capability. Singapore offered mature bonded warehousing, international logistics and engineering outsourcing resources, but the team lacked hands-on setup experience — internal estimates put independent setup at at least 12 months.

"Our technology works at home, but overseas customers want a solution that fits their production-line and warehouse rhythm — not a lone forklift sitting in a corner."

— Blake Zhang
