From China to the Middle East and Europe — A Global Expansion Anchored in Singapore

An EV drive-system components manufacturer wanted to move beyond reliance on trading agents, using Singapore as an operational and compliance anchor to simultaneously build a Middle East distribution network and break into European OEM supply chains. Here is the full story of that global expansion.

First dual-market delivery 8 months (originally estimated at 18 months) · 15 overseas distribution / service partners · 2 regional markets entered simultaneously

Background and Challenge

The company focuses on R&D and manufacturing of EV motors and control systems. Its overseas business had previously relied on trading agents for sporadic exports — leaving it without direct relationships with end customers, and unable to build stable after-sales capability. Leadership determined that to truly enter the global supply chain, the company needed two parallel capabilities: a distribution and project network for the Middle East, and OEM certification with long-term supply capacity for Europe.

Singapore, with its internationally recognized legal and financial systems, is also a commercial hub trusted by both Middle Eastern and European customers — the team positioned it as the operational and compliance anchor for this dual-track expansion. But the two markets differ entirely in certification standards, trade finance structures and customer development paths. Tackling them independently, the internal estimate was at least 18 months to gain traction in both.

"The Middle East wants distribution networks and project speed. Europe wants certification and long-term stable supply. The playbooks are completely different, and we didn't have people who understood both."

— Bruce Dong

Solution and Outcomes

01

The GlobalX team assessed Singapore's role as a dual-track hub for the Middle East and Europe, mapping out European CE certification pathways alongside the local qualifications required for Middle East market access.

Local Resource Matching GlobalX

Connected the company with local trade finance, export credit insurance and international logistics solutions in Singapore, designing separate funding structures for Middle East project prepayments and Europe's long supply cycles.

03

04

Dual-Market Rollout in Parallel

The Singapore operations hub simultaneously coordinated Middle East distribution network buildout and certification plus delivery of the first European orders.
