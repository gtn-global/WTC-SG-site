At Beyondsoft's Singapore Hub, How a Chinese Tech Company Turned "Going Global" into Three Coordinates

01 Global Business Opportunity 2025 – 2026

Before visiting Beyondsoft's Singapore hub, my impression of the company was still stuck on "a Shenzhen-listed IT services outsourcing firm." On the ground, I realised that view was outdated — they have turned their "Global Innovation Center" plan into three real coordinates: Tokyo, Beijing, and Singapore. Not a plaque on the wall, but actual incubation of different application cases in each of the three locations. The Singapore node carries the AI + finance, smart manufacturing, and green energy tracks.

I was received by the Beyondsoft Singapore team. The conversation was not the usual press-release style — "how big we are, how fast we are growing" — but rather how a specific client need becomes a replicable solution. This "start from one concrete project and push outward" narrative is quite different from most going-global companies I meet, which tend to lead with strategy and follow with cases. Beyondsoft does it the other way around.

"I have seen many Chinese companies' regional centers in Singapore; a lot of them are essentially 'sales outposts' — people sent over, but R&D and decision-making still back home. This Singapore center feels different. It is more like they genuinely placed part of the 'innovation' itself in Singapore, not just the selling."

— Dong Lixin, Secretary-General & COO, WTC Singapore

Public records show that Beyondsoft International (Singapore) has recently been advancing the sale of its U.S. subsidiary's equity — not something we discussed on this visit, but something I noticed later while reading public filings. Put together, you can sense that this company's moves on the global board follow a coherent logic, not opportunistic, single-point expansion.

This exchange was never aimed at closing a deal on the spot, yet it gave me a useful reference point for judging how Chinese tech companies actually land overseas.

Notes by Bruce Dong
Secretary-General & COO, WTC Singapore
