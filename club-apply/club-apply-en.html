About WTC ONE Club

What is WTC ONE Club?

WTC ONE Club is part of the World Trade Center Association — one of the world's largest international networks of business leaders. The club is built on three core pillars: Global Business Opportunities (high-level networking and business matchmaking), Capital for Expanding Globally (connecting international capital to accelerate internationalization), and Think Tank for Decision Making (top-tier advisory and market intelligence). We believe the business relationships that matter most are the ones curated by a few, not scaled by many.

Why join?

Joining WTC ONE Club means becoming part of the founding circle building Singapore's newest trust network from the ground up. You'll connect directly with WTCA's global network of 300+ nodes, as a core member personally vetted and invited by the WTC Singapore council — whether you're deepening your local footprint or accelerating international expansion, every connection here is curated, not casual.

How do I become a member?

Complete the application form below, sharing your background and what you hope to achieve through WTC ONE Club. Once approved by the council, our team will reach out directly to arrange further conversation — see the three steps below for the full process.
