02 — About WTC Singapore
Rooted in Asia-Pacific, a hub linking global
markets with an industrial-capital ecosystem
Gateway from Asia-Pacific to the World · NEXUS of Capital, Innovation and Global Markets

World Trade Center Singapore (WTC Singapore for short) is the sole licensed member of the World Trade Centers Association (WTCA) Global Network in Singapore—an international business and investment platform based in Singapore, serving global enterprises and committed to connecting international trade, industry cooperation, capital, and innovation resources.

WTC Singapore is built around three business pillars: the WTC ONE Club, the GlobalX Going-Global Accelerator, and the WTC Fund international investment fund. It continuously connects capital with projects and technology with industry, advancing international exchange, industrial synergy, and enterprise globalization, and deepening the integration of ASEAN, China, and global markets.

WTC One Club
Cross-border Business Community
A premium business community of global entrepreneurs, investors, and industry leaders, fostering cross-border cooperation and resource matching through private events and member networks.
GlobalX Going-Global Accelerator
Market Access One-Stop Service Platform
A one-stop acceleration service for going-global enterprises—market entry, compliant landing, and local operations—bridging ASEAN and China in both directions.
WTC Fund International Capital & Innovation Ecosystem
Innovation Capital Investment Fund
